Google Pay

Wallet tokenized via Braintree. In sandbox it uses Google Pay's TEST environment.

In the TEST environment Google Pay returns test instruments and does not require a Merchant ID. It works on Chrome. For production you need the Google Merchant ID in .env.

Apple Pay

Wallet tokenized via Braintree. Available only on Safari / Apple devices.

Apple Pay requires: HTTPS, a verified domain in the Braintree Control Panel, Safari and an Apple device with a card in the Wallet. It is not available on localhost over HTTP.
