Braintree integration demo — best practices

Three families of payment methods, each with the guest, checkout with vault and returning customer flows. Node.js/Express backend, braintree-web v3 client SDK.

sandbox environment · demo amount … . Use the test cards listed in the README. No real charges.

1 · Card (Hosted Fields) + 3DS

PCI-compliant card data collection (SAQ A) with hosted fields, 3D Secure 2 authentication and anti-fraud device data.

Guest Vault Returning

2 · PayPal

One-time PayPal checkout, saving to the Vault (billing agreement) and reusing the saved method for the returning customer.

Checkout Vault Returning

3 · Google Pay & Apple Pay

Wallet pass-through with Braintree tokenization. Apple Pay requires HTTPS + a verified domain (see notes in the README).
